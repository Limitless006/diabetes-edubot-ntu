# 03. Prevention, Self-Care, and Management of Type 2 Diabetes
# الوقاية، الرعاية الذاتية، والإدارة اليومية لمرض السكري من النوع الثاني

## 1. Evidence-Based Prevention: The Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP) | الوقاية المثبتة علمياً
Extensive clinical trials, led by the landmark **National Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP)** sponsored by the NIH and CDC, prove that Type 2 diabetes can be prevented or significantly delayed in high-risk individuals and those with prediabetes through structured lifestyle interventions:

1. **Targeted Weight Reduction (فقدان 5% إلى 7% من وزن الجسم):**
   * Achieving a modest weight loss of **5% to 7%** of initial body weight reduces the risk of developing Type 2 diabetes by **58%** in the general adult population, and by **71%** in adults aged 60 years and older.
   * *Example:* For a person weighing 200 pounds (91 kg), losing 10 to 14 pounds (4.5 to 6.3 kg) provides transformative metabolic protection.
2. **Regular Moderate Physical Activity (150 دقيقة أسبوعياً من النشاط البدني):**
   * Engaging in at least **150 minutes per week** of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity (e.g., 30 minutes a day, 5 days per week, such as brisk walking, cycling, water aerobics, or active dancing).
   * Incorporating 2 to 3 weekly sessions of muscle-strengthening resistance exercises (resistance bands, bodyweight exercises, or light weights) to enhance skeletal muscle glucose uptake.
3. **Nutritional Quality (التغذية المتوازنة):**
   * Prioritizing unprocessed foods, high-fiber legumes, whole grains, vegetables, and lean proteins while eliminating sugar-sweetened beverages and ultra-processed snacks.

## 2. Practical Nutrition: The Diabetes Plate Method | التغذية السريرية وطريقة الطبق
The American Diabetes Association (ADA) and CDC recommend the **Diabetes Plate Method** as a visual, practical tool to manage portions and macronutrient distribution without tedious calorie counting:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------+
|                       THE PLATE                       |
|                                                       |
|   +-----------------------+   +-------------------+   |
|   |                       |   |   LEAN PROTEINS   |   |
|   |                       |   |       (25%)       |   |
|   |  NON-STARCHY VEGGIES  |   +-------------------+   |
|   |         (50%)         |   |   QUALITY CARBS   |   |
|   |                       |   |       (25%)       |   |
|   +-----------------------+   +-------------------+   |
|                                                       |
|   BEVERAGE: Pure Water, Unsweetened Tea or Coffee     |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Half the Plate (50%) — Non-Starchy Vegetables (خضروات غير نشوية):**
   * Broccoli, spinach, leafy greens, green beans, cauliflower, cucumbers, bell peppers, tomatoes, and cabbage. Rich in dietary fiber, vitamins, and minerals with minimal impact on blood glucose spikes.
2. **One-Quarter of the Plate (25%) — Lean Protein Foods (بروتينات قليلة الدهون):**
   * Skinless poultry (chicken, turkey), fish (salmon, tuna, cod), eggs, tofu, legumes (lentils, chickpeas), and lean beef. Protein promotes satiety and slows carbohydrate absorption.
3. **One-Quarter of the Plate (25%) — Quality Carbohydrate Foods (كربوهيدرات معقدة غنية بالألياف):**
   * Whole grains (brown rice, whole oats, quinoa, whole-wheat bread), starchy vegetables (sweet potatoes, green peas, corn), and whole fresh fruits. High-fiber carbohydrates digest slowly, avoiding sharp glycemic surges.
4. **Hydration (السوائل والترطيب):**
   * Choose water as the primary beverage. Avoid sodas, sweetened iced teas, energy drinks, and fruit juices that cause immediate glycemic spikes.

## 3. The "ABCs" of Diabetes Management | قاعدة الـ ABCs للسيطرة على السكري وتجنب المضاعفات
The CDC, MedlinePlus, and NHS emphasize the "ABCs" framework to protect blood vessels and vital organs from long-term diabetic damage:

| Letter | Medical Component | Standard Target for Most Adults | Clinical Significance |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | **A1C (السكر التراكمي)** | **Below 7.0%** (individualized) | Reflects average glucose over 2-3 months. Every 1% drop in A1C substantially cuts the risk of kidney failure, blindness, and nerve damage. Checked every 3 to 6 months. |
| **B** | **Blood Pressure (ضغط الدم)** | **Below 130/80 mmHg** | High blood pressure strains blood vessels and compounds the risk of heart attacks, stroke, and progressive nephropathy (kidney disease). |
| **C** | **Cholesterol (الكوليسترول)** | **LDL < 70-100 mg/dL** | Managing low-density lipoprotein (LDL "bad" cholesterol) and triglycerides prevents atherosclerotic plaque buildup and arterial occlusion. |
| **S** | **Stop Smoking (الإقلاع عن التدخين)** | **Zero tobacco use** | Smoking severely constricts blood vessels, exacerbates poor circulation in the extremities, and doubles the risk of premature heart disease. |

* **Verified Sources:**

## 4. Routine Self-Care & Preventative Screenings | الفحوصات الروتينية والرعاية الذاتية اليومية

### A. Daily Foot Care (العناية اليومية بالقدمين)
Because diabetic neuropathy diminishes pain sensation and peripheral vascular disease impedes blood supply, minor blisters can develop into severe non-healing ulcers:
* **Inspect daily:** Check soles, heels, and between toes for redness, cuts, blisters, swelling, calluses, or nail abnormalities using a mirror or family member's assistance.
* **Wash & dry:** Wash feet daily in lukewarm water; pat dry gently, paying meticulous attention to drying completely between the toes.
* **Moisturize:** Apply lotion to the tops and bottoms of the feet to prevent skin cracking, but **never apply lotion between the toes** (moisture fosters fungal growth).
* **Footwear safety:** Never walk barefoot, even indoors. Always wear clean, dry socks and well-fitting, supportive shoes. Shake shoes out before wearing to check for foreign objects.
* **Nails:** Cut toenails straight across and file edges gently. Never perform bathroom surgery or cut calluses with razor blades.

### B. Annual Dilated Eye Exam (فحص قاع العين السنوي الموسع)
* Early diabetic retinopathy causes microaneurysms and capillary leakage in the retina without causing early visual symptoms.
* An annual dilated pupil examination by an optometrist or ophthalmologist detects microvascular changes before irreversible vision loss occurs.

### C. Renal Protection (مراقبة وظائف الكلى)
* Annual urine test for **microalbumin / Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (UACR)** to detect early glomerular leakage.
* Annual blood test for **serum creatinine and eGFR** (estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate).

### D. Dental & Oral Health (صحة الأسنان واللثة)
* Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and attend semi-annual dental check-ups. Hyperglycemia increases oral bacteria and gum disease (periodontitis), which in turn worsens systemic glycemic control.

### E. Preventative Vaccinations (اللقاحات الوقائية)
* Annual influenza (flu) vaccine, pneumococcal pneumonia vaccine, Hepatitis B series, and COVID-19 boosters as recommended by public health guidelines.

## 5. Educational Overview of Pharmacotherapy Classes | نظرة تثقيفية عامة على فئات الأدوية
*(Educational guidance only — Strictly NO prescribing, dosing, or brand endorsements)*

When lifestyle modifications alone do not maintain blood glucose within individual target ranges, healthcare providers prescribe oral or injectable medications:

1. **Biguanides (Metformin / ميتفورمين):**
   * *Mechanism:* First-line oral therapy. Decreases glucose production in the liver (gluconeogenesis), increases peripheral muscle insulin sensitivity, and slightly delays intestinal glucose absorption. Does not typically cause hypoglycemia or weight gain.
2. **Sulfonylureas (سلفونيل يوريا — e.g., Glimepiride, Glipizide):**
   * *Mechanism:* Stimulates pancreatic beta cells to produce and secrete additional insulin throughout the day. May carry a risk of hypoglycemia and mild weight gain.
3. **SGLT2 Inhibitors (مثبطات SGLT2 — e.g., Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin):**
   * *Mechanism:* Blocks the Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter 2 in the renal proximal tubules, causing excess glucose to be excreted in the urine. Proven to provide significant cardiovascular protection and slow progression of chronic kidney disease.
4. **DPP-4 Inhibitors (مثبطات DPP-4 — e.g., Sitagliptin, Linagliptin):**
   * *Mechanism:* Inhibits the dipeptidyl peptidase-4 enzyme, prolonging the lifespan of natural incretin hormones (GLP-1 and GIP) which stimulate insulin production in a glucose-dependent manner.
5. **GLP-1 Receptor Agonists & Dual Incretins (مقلدات GLP-1 — e.g., Semaglutide, Liraglutide, Tirzepatide):**
   * *Mechanism:* Injected or oral medications that mimic gut incretin hormones, stimulating insulin release when blood sugar rises, suppressing glucagon, delaying gastric emptying, and promoting satiety and significant weight loss. Demonstrated major cardiovascular and renal benefits.
6. **Insulin Therapy (العلاج بالإنسولين):**
   * *Mechanism:* When pancreatic beta cells lose the capacity to manufacture sufficient endogenous insulin, replacement therapy with basal (long-acting) and/or bolus (rapid-acting) insulin is introduced.

> **CRITICAL MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:**
> All medications, dosing regimens, and therapeutic combinations must be prescribed and monitored solely by a licensed physician or specialist. Patients must never alter dosages or discontinue medications based on chatbot conversations.
